## Book Project

**Race to Go Green: Three Pathways in Green Industrial Revolution**

Based on my doctoral dissertation, this book project examines how political elites, institutional structures, and geopolitical pressures shape green industrial transformation in Korea, Japan, and Taiwan.

---

## Working Papers

### Green Coalitions and Strategic Reorientation in Northeast Asia

This article develops a comparative framework explaining variation in green industrial strategies across Korea, Japan, and Taiwan through the interaction of external shocks, elite paradigm shifts, and agenda-setting institutions.

---

### Rare Earth Governance and Industrial Strategy in China

Co-authoring with Joo-Youn Jung.

This project examines the evolution of China's rare earth governance regime and its implications for industrial policy, economic security, and global supply chains.

---

### Green Industrial Transformation in Japan and Taiwan

This project compares how Taiwan and Japan have responded to the dual pressures of decarbonization and geopolitical competition through industrial policy, energy transition, and technological upgrading.
